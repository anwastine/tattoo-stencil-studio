How it works

The service runs on credits. One credit produces one stencil. New accounts receive 29 free credits on first sign-in with Google — no payment details required.

Credit price

Additional credits cost ₹9 per credit. All prices are in Indian Rupees. We are not registered for GST, so no GST is charged and none is collected.

Payment methods

Payments are processed by Razorpay and support UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking and wallets. We never see or store your card or UPI details.

Validity

Credits do not expire. They can only be used on this website, cannot be transferred to another account, and cannot be exchanged for cash.
