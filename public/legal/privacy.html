Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

What we collect

  • Account details from Google: your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier. We receive these only after you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Credit and payment records: your credit balance, and a record of purchases and usage. Payments are processed by Razorpay — we never receive or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Technical data: basic request information such as IP address, used to prevent abuse of the free-credit offer.

Your photographs

A downsized copy of the photograph you upload is sent to our AI provider (OpenAI or Google) solely to generate your stencil. We do not store your uploaded photograph or the generated stencil on our servers — the images live only in your browser for the duration of your session. Our providers process the image under their own API terms and do not use API content to train their models.

Why we hold it

To operate your account, keep an accurate credit balance, provide support, meet tax and accounting obligations, and prevent fraud and abuse.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers needed to run the site: Vercel (hosting), Neon (database), Razorpay (payments), and OpenAI or Google (image generation). We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

Cookies

We set a single essential cookie to keep you signed in. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.
