Cancellation and refunds

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Failed generations are free

If a stencil fails to generate for any technical reason, the credit is returned to your balance automatically and immediately. You are never charged for a stencil you did not receive.

Unused credits

You may request a refund of unused credits within 7 days of purchase. Email team@techwalo.com from the address on your account with the payment reference. Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days; the corresponding credits are removed from your balance.

Used credits

Credits already spent on generated stencils are not refundable, because the artwork has been delivered. If you are unhappy with a result, contact us — we will usually add replacement credits so you can try again.

Cancellation

There is no subscription and nothing recurring to cancel. You buy credits only when you choose to. You may stop using the service and request deletion of your account at any time by writing to team@techwalo.com.

Disputes

If a payment was debited but credits were not added, write to team@techwalo.com with the payment reference. We reconcile against Razorpay and credit or refund within 3 business days.
