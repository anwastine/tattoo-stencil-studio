Delivery policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Digital delivery

Tattoo Stencil Studio sells a digital service. There are no physical goods and nothing is shipped.

How and when you receive your order

  • Credits are added to your account immediately after a successful payment, normally within a few seconds.
  • Stencils are generated in your browser session, usually within 30 seconds to 4 minutes depending on the output size, and are downloaded directly as a PNG file.

If credits do not appear within 10 minutes of a successful payment, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] with the payment reference and we will resolve it.
